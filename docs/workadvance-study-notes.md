# WorkAdvance 2020 source notes

## Primary source

Schaberg and Greenberg (MDRC, March 2020), *Long-Term Effects of a Sectoral Advancement Strategy: Costs, Benefits, and Impacts from the WorkAdvance Demonstration*. The canonical source URL is `https://www.mdrc.org/work/publications/long-term-effects-sectoral-advancement-strategy`.

The report evaluates four provider-specific implementations between June 2011 and June 2013 using random assignment. It is a single multi-site study, not four independent replications: Per Scholas (Bronx, NY; information technology; N=690), St. Nicks Alliance (Brooklyn, NY; environmental remediation; N=479), Madison Strategies Group (Tulsa, OK; transportation/manufacturing; N=697), and Towards Employment (northeast Ohio; health care/manufacturing; N=698). The full randomized sample is 2,564 (1,293 WorkAdvance, 1,271 control).

## First publication scope

Publish one Article, one umbrella Initiative, four provider/site Initiatives, design, pooled 2018 earnings and $30,000-or-more results, four provider-specific 2018 earnings results, four provider-specific high-earnings results, and three source-modelled projected net-financial-gain observations for each provider. Every factual result must retain Article and provider-initiative links. A dashboard may pool only where it filters the Article and understands that provider heterogeneity is masked.

The monetary benefit-cost observations are source-modelled **net financial gains or losses**, not benefit-cost ratios. They are per participant, in 2018 dollars, inflation-adjusted, discounted to 2018 present values at 3.5%, and include monetized nonmarket time and deadweight loss. Projection horizons differ: Per Scholas seven years, St. Nicks Alliance ten years, and Madison Strategies Group/Towards Employment remain at the 62-month observation period because the report says the positive earnings impacts appeared to disappear. Do not rank sites from these values, convert them to a benefit-cost ratio, or omit the perspective/horizon.

## Source values selected

Table ES.3 pooled 2018 outcomes: total earnings +$2,716 (26,419 versus 23,703; p=.003); earnings of $30,000 or more +6.4 percentage points (40.0% versus 33.7%; p=.001); N=2,564. Table ES.2 provider-specific 2018 earnings differences: Per Scholas +$6,281 (p=.004), St. Nicks Alliance +$2,849 (p=.208), Madison Strategies Group +$787 (p=.603), Towards Employment +$1,404 (p=.275). The corresponding $30,000-or-more differences are +6.3 points (p=.099), +9.3 (p=.033), +5.8 (p=.092), and +5.1 (p=.117).

Table ES.4 projected/observation-period net financial gains, respectively participant / government budget / society: Per Scholas $15,456 / $13,387 / $31,387; St. Nicks Alliance $9,387 / $3,660 / $13,742; Madison Strategies Group $11,192 / $1,615 / $13,114; Towards Employment $5,505 / $265 / $5,820. These are not raw program operating costs and cannot be compared to unadjusted cost observations elsewhere in the dashboard.

## Mapping decisions

Use `Decimal estimate` for effects and net gains; `Integer sample size`; `P value` text; `Price year` 2018; `Currency` USD; `unit` text; `locator` text; factual checkbox; source and Related relations. A generic `Cost` decimal is deliberately not used for net gains. Geography will reuse existing cross-space canonical City/State entities only after all-space discovery and verification; no location or provider identity is created from the report’s narrative alone.
