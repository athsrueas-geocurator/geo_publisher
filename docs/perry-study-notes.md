## Modeling issues to preserve

- The first column of Table 1 changes meaning between panels: IRR rows vary tax deadweight loss; benefit/cost rows vary the discount rate and all fix deadweight loss at 50%. Do not copy one axis across both panels.
- IRRs and their SEs are percentages/percentage points in the source; fractional-rate publication requires scaling both. Benefit/cost ratios and their SEs are dimensionless ratios. A generic SE property's display format must not be changed globally to percent because Saga and STAR use other units.
- Individual and social returns have different benefit scopes. Crime valuation changes social scenarios; it is not an individual-return parameter. The source's missing individual benefit/cost cells are unknown/not reported, not zero.
- Pooled returns come from pooled profiles, not an arithmetic average of sex-specific rates. These are related estimates, not independent experiments or confidence bounds.
- The cost is undiscounted 2006 USD per child for the program, including operations and capital. Do not call it annual expenditure or treat it as the financing-adjusted cost denominator for all scenarios. Cost price year differs from STAR's 1998 USD.
- Table 2 reports descriptive group means. Subsequent reassignment compromised initial randomization; subtracting those means does not by itself reproduce an adjusted causal effect or its SE. Original group counts total 123 but do not establish every outcome's complete-case denominator.
- Lifetime economic calculations include imputed missing earnings and projections after age 40 through age 65. Preserve measured versus modeled records and link them to the same underlying study; outcomes beyond observed follow-up are not measured lifetime earnings.

Before publishing, resolve program/study, Ypsilanti/Michigan, publication/version, subgroup and scenario-property identities across spaces. Reuse existing IRR, discount, cost, price-year and citation properties where their semantics fit. The prior exact-title search is not proof of global absence. Preserve stable IDs and publish via the existing journaled proposal and bounty-link workflow, with short descriptions and explanatory blocks.
