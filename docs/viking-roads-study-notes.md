# Viking ROADS Demonstration, 2025

Primary source: Stanley Dai, Kayla Warner, and Colleen Sommo, *Charting Pathways: Three-Year Findings from the Viking ROADS Demonstration* (MDRC, March 2025), https://www.mdrc.org/work/publications/charting-pathways/file-full.

Viking Resources for Obtaining Associate Degrees and Success (Viking ROADS) began at SUNY Westchester in 2018 as a close replication of CUNY ASAP. It offered a three-year package of dedicated academic counseling, tutoring and career services, a summer orientation, a tuition-and-fees gap scholarship, monthly transportation vouchers, a $500 annual textbook stipend, and reserved seats in selected first-year courses. Eligibility and requirements matter: eligible New York residents were first-time students or qualifying transfers with at most 18 credits and GPA at least 2.0, eligible majors, full-time enrollment willingness, and no more than two developmental-course recommendations; participants had monthly counselor/support-service requirements.

The evaluation randomized 574 eligible, consenting students in fall 2019, fall 2020, and spring 2021: 288 program and 286 control. Controls could access all existing SUNY Westchester services but were not eligible for Viking ROADS. The report identifies the confirmatory three-year outcome as any degree or certificate at any college; transcript and National Student Clearinghouse data underpin the academic outcomes. COVID-19 changed delivery and learning conditions: the first cohort moved remote after one semester, later cohorts entered during largely online instruction, and support moved to virtual or hybrid formats.
