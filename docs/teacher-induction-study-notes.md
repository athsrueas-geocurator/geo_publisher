# Comprehensive teacher-induction evaluation notes

Primary report downloaded and extracted on 2026-09-12: Glazerman et al., *Impacts of Comprehensive Teacher Induction: Final Results from a Randomized Controlled Study*, NCEE 2010-4027. Local source copy: `tmp/teacher-induction-20104027.pdf`; official report URL: `https://nces.ed.gov/sites/default/files/migrated/nces_pubs/ncee/pubs/20104027/pdf/20104027.pdf`.

The trial randomized 1,009 beginning teachers in 418 schools across 17 districts. It compares comprehensive induction with each district's usual induction, and separates one-year from two-year districts; those strata must not be pooled as if program duration was randomized.

Table V.5 reports the two-year-district, year-three benchmark results: reading effect size 0.11 (p=.018; 1,347 students, 74 teachers, six districts) and math effect size 0.20 (p<.001; 1,198 students, 68 teachers, six districts). The report says the estimates depend on the current-and-prior-test analysis sample; removing the prior-test requirement yields no significant reading or math impact. The math result remains positive in seven of ten alternative specifications; the reading result is more sensitive.

Tables D.9 and D.10 report no statistically significant three-year retention impact. In two-year districts, the adjusted treatment-control differences were 7.1 percentage points for same-school retention (p=.159), 4.0 for same-district retention (p=.388), and -0.7 for retention in teaching (p=.850). These are outcomes from the same trial, not independent studies.
