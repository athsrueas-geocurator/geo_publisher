# Teacher Incentive Fund randomized evaluation notes

Primary report extracted 2026-09-12: Chiang et al., *Evaluation of the Teacher Incentive Fund: Final Report on Implementation and Impacts of Pay-for-Performance Across Four Years*, NCEE 2018-4004. Official PDF: `https://ies.ed.gov/sites/default/files/migrated/nces_pubs/ncee/pubs/20184004/pdf/20184004.pdf`.

The impact sample contains 131 schools in ten districts. Treatment schools received the pay-for-performance component; control schools received the same broader compensation system but an across-the-board one-percent bonus instead. The package evaluates the bonus component, not an isolated teacher bonus in every setting.

Table VI.4 reports achievement effects in student z-score units. Year 3 has the clearest joint pair: math +0.06 SD (p=.02; N=39,770) and reading +0.04 SD (p=.02; N=39,538). Year 1 reading is +0.03 SD (p=.04), Year 2 reading +0.03 SD (p=.02), and later math effects range from +0.02 to +0.04 with reported p-values .33, .07, .02, and .13. The report does not print standard errors in this table; do not invent them.

The study's four annual rows are repeated horizons from one multisite experiment. A dashboard may compare timepoints, but must not count each as independent evidence. A future cost-effectiveness representation must preserve the report's comparison assumptions and is not an observed benefit-cost ratio.
