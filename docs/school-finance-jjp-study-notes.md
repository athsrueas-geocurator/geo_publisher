## Extraction plan

The design uses event studies and instrumental variables based on court-mandated finance reforms. The intervention is sustained school spending exposure, not a single provider's uniform program or randomized one-off grant. Build the Study with linked reform/spending context and childhood-income groups. Preserve distinct observation counts (individuals, families, person-years), not one ambiguous N.

Table IV (printed 198 / PDF page 43) includes adult log-wage and log-family-income regressions for all children and childhood-income subgroups. Select the preferred 2SLS specification and preserve its coefficient scale and clustered SE. A log-log coefficient is not a percentage-point effect; approximate 10% spending interpretations must be labeled as derived or author-interpreted, with the transformation explicit.

The benefit-cost discussion spans printed 212–213 / PDF pages 57–58. It uses a hypothetical birth cohort, historical spending in 2013 dollars, 12 years of increased spending, discounting and projected adult earnings. Treat this as a suggestive model, not an observed program-cost ledger. The wage percentages differ between adjoining paragraphs, and rounded benefit/cost summaries do not directly reproduce the stated rounded ratio. Inspect visually and reconcile timing, rounding and assumptions before constructing a computed dashboard ratio.
