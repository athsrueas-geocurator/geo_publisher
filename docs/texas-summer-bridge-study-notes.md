# Texas Developmental Summer Bridge Programs, 2011

Primary source: Heather D. Wathington et al., *Getting Ready for College: An Implementation and Early Impacts Study of Eight Texas Developmental Summer Bridge Programs* (NCPR/MDRC, October 2011), https://www.mdrc.org/work/publications/getting-ready-college/file-full.

The 2009 experimental evaluation enrolled 1,318 consenting students at eight Texas institutions: seven community colleges and one open-admissions four-year university. Students were randomly assigned to program eligibility or their college’s regular services. The programs shared accelerated developmental instruction, academic support, college-knowledge content, and a possible $400 completion stipend, but varied by site; this is a multi-site program model rather than one uniform institution-level intervention.

The source reports average resources required of **$1,319 per admitted student in 2009 dollars** across the eight sites, with site averages ranging $840–$2,349 and total costs $62,633–$296,033. It says some costs may be start-up costs and that three-year amortization would lower the average. The early findings are only one academic year. The source reports no statistically significant fall or spring enrollment impact, but improved attempts/passing in selected college-level math and writing outcomes and one additional college-level credit attempted in the 2009–2010 academic year.
