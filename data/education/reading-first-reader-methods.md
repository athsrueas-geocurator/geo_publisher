## Study design and interpretation

This evaluation combines 17 regression-discontinuity sites and one site with group random assignment. Effects of Reading First funding are adjusted for site ratings and selected covariates, account for clustering, and are averaged across sites in proportion to their number of funded sample schools. The final sample contains 248 schools—125 funded and 123 unfunded—in 13 states. Schools were selected near local funding cut-points; the sample is not nationally representative, and the school count is not the student sample size for each outcome.

SAT 10 comprehension results pool spring 2005, 2006 and 2007. TOSWRF decoding was measured only in first grade in spring 2007. Classroom observations of instruction and engagement, and surveys of implementation, measure different outcomes from student achievement. The survey findings also depend on self-report and response patterns; reading-coach response rates differed between funded and unfunded schools.

Estimates and uncertainty use Appendix D's reported precision where available. Standard errors and confidence bounds share the estimate's unit, and intervals have 95% confidence. P-values are two-tailed. An interval containing zero indicates uncertainty about the effect, not proof of exactly no effect. Rounding can place an interval endpoint at zero even when the report marks the estimate statistically significant.

Native-unit and standardized results are two representations of the same outcome contrast. They are not independent findings. The standardization samples differ across measures, so an SD label alone does not make the results comparable. Percentage-point differences, proportion differences and percentage changes are distinct quantities.

In the source tables, the funded-school mean is observed and unadjusted; the mean without funding is an estimated counterfactual. Their rounded difference can differ from the reported impact. The eight selected survey outcomes report p-values but do not include standard errors or confidence intervals in the selected exhibits.

Coverage is limited to Exhibits 2.1–2.6 and D.1–D.4, rather than every subgroup, yearly or robustness analysis. Matched program-delivery costs are unavailable in this dataset, so these results do not support a cost-effectiveness ranking.
